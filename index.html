    How to play

    The board

    The board is a big tic-tac-toe grid. Each of its nine big squares holds a small tic-tac-toe board.

    123 456 789

    Squares are numbered 1 to 9, row by row. Big squares and small squares use the same numbers. A move is written as two numbers: 4-1 means big square 4, small square 1.

    Taking turns

    X goes first and can play anywhere. After that, the small square you pick decides where the other player goes next. If you play in small square 1, they must play in big square 1.

    X plays 4-1, the top left small square of big square 4. O must now play in big square 1.

    The board shows where you can play. Those big squares are outlined in the colour of the player to move, and the rest are dimmed.

    Winning a big square

    Get three in a row on a small board to win that big square. A won square is closed, so nobody can play in it again.

    If a small board fills up with no winner, it is a tie. That board is wiped clean and stays open.

    Free moves

    If you are sent to a big square that is already won, you get a free move. The Free move setting decides where it can go.

    • Anywhere. You can play in any open big square.
    • Touching squares. You can only play in open big squares that touch the one you were sent to, diagonals included. If none of them is open, you can play in any open big square.

    You can change this setting during a game.

    Winning the game

    Win three big squares in a row. If all nine big squares are won and nobody has a row, the game is a draw.

    Playing the computer

    Set Opponent to Computer, then pick a level and a side. X moves first. Undo takes back your last move and the computer's reply.

    Keyboard

    Use the arrow keys to move around the board. Press Enter or Space to play in the square you are on.
